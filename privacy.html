Privacy Policy

Personal Automation Tool. Last updated: 16 September 2026.

Scope

This tool is a private, self-hosted utility with a single user: its owner. It is not offered to any other person and collects no data from anyone else.

Data accessed

When the owner authorises it, the tool accesses data belonging to the owner's own accounts, solely in order to carry out tasks the owner has asked it to perform. It accesses nothing beyond what those tasks require.

Storage

Credentials and any retained data are stored locally on hardware controlled by the owner, readable only by the owner's own system account. Nothing is copied to any external service, analytics platform or database.

Sharing

Data is never sold, rented, transferred or disclosed to any third party, and is never used for advertising, profiling, or for training or improving any generalised artificial-intelligence or machine-learning model. Data is transmitted only between the owner's own system and the official APIs of the services concerned, over encrypted connections.

Limited use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

The owner may revoke access at any time from their account security settings (for Google accounts, myaccount.google.com/permissions), which immediately stops all further access. Locally stored credentials and data may be deleted by the owner at any time.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with the date above revised accordingly.
