Hermes Personal Assistant

A self-hosted personal automation assistant for a single user.

What this app does

Hermes Personal Assistant is a private, self-hosted assistant that runs on the owner's own server. It is operated by and used by one single person - its owner - and is not offered as a service to any other user.

It helps its owner with everyday personal administration by connecting to that same owner's Google account:

  • Gmail - read, search and send the owner's own mail, and save attachments such as statements and receipts.
  • Google Calendar - create, read and update the owner's own events and reminders.
  • Google Drive - store and retrieve the owner's own files, including automated weekly backups of the assistant's configuration.
  • Google Docs / Sheets - read and write the owner's own documents and spreadsheets for personal bookkeeping and reports.
  • Google Tasks - manage the owner's own to-do items.
  • Google Contacts (read only) - look up the owner's own contacts when composing mail or invitations.

How it works

The owner authorises the application once through Google's standard OAuth 2.0 consent flow. The resulting credentials are stored only on the owner's own server and are used solely to act on the owner's behalf, at the owner's request. There is no shared backend, no multi-user account system, and no third party involved.
